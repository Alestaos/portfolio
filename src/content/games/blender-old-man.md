## What it is

My first character render, made while teaching myself Blender. He's a deliberately blocky,
stylised old man: a broad, top-heavy body, stubby legs with oversized boots, small inset eyes
and a round "O" of a mouth, plus a ring on one hand as a small character detail.

## Where it's at

This is a **pre-materials** render. The model is shown in plain clay grey and rendered in
Blender's Eevee engine, so the focus is entirely on form, proportion and lighting before any
colour or texture is added. A single hard key light picks out the chunky geometry and throws
strong shadows across the body to show off the silhouette.

## Next steps

He's a work in progress. Next comes materials and texturing, then more of the Blender
pipeline as I learn it. This page will be updated as he develops.

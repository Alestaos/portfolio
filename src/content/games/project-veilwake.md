## What it is

A dialogue sample from **Project Veilwake**, my game concept for the Narrative Design module of my
MSc in Computing (Games & XR). This piece covers Stage 2 of the hero's journey, the Call to
Adventure. Kaidan, a stressed student, ends up in the Prismwilds, the domain of Laxis, a talking
cat who can’t hold a thought for long.

I storyboarded the scene shot by shot and then cut it into an animatic.

## How it was made

- **Storyboard:** 24 shots across scenes 2.0 to 2.8. Every panel notes its action, FX, framing,
  dialogue and the player's choices.
- **Branching:** the player picks Kaidan's replies (honest, silent, joking, curious and others),
  and each choice points to a follow-up scene. Only one branch reveals Laxis’s
  name and domain.
- **The world reacts:** the aurora skybox follows Laxis’s mood. Pink bursts mean Laxis is
  distracted, and the sky calms back to violet on refocusing.
- **Animatic:** the panels are timed into a 3-minute 20-second cut, with subtitled dialogue and
  callouts for the motion still to come (preening, ears drooping, eyes changing colour).

## The brief

A volunteer project for the **Association of Irish Powerchair Football (AIPF)**. Clubs often
rely on young members, from children to teenagers, to run their social media. The AIPF wanted
simple visual guides those members could follow to make posts that look professional and
consistent with the AIPF brand.

## The approach

Two portrait infographics, each covering one topic, sized 9:16 so they can be shared and read
on a phone, where the audience will actually use them.

**Social Dimensions and Video Safe Zones.** Each standard post size (square, portrait,
widescreen and reels/stories) is drawn to scale as its own tile, labelled with its pixel
dimensions and the platforms it's used on. The aspect ratios are shown rather than just listed.
Below, a phone mockup with dashed guides shows where the username and captions cover the
screen, so the key message and call to action stay in the safe area.

**Branding and Consistency.** The official AIPF colour palette is shown with hex and RGB values
ready to copy. Two side-by-side examples show a correct logo and a pixelated, distorted one.
A pair of example match posters is annotated to show what makes a set of posts look like they
belong together: colour palette, typography, relevant imagery and overall visual consistency.

Throughout, the style is friendly and bold: rounded display type, big tick and cross
indicators, and illustrated wheelchair football players. Young readers can take in the rules
at a glance instead of reading a style guide.

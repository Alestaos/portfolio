## The brief

Design a logo for **BitBoi Games**, an imagined game company, and show how it holds up as a
real brand across different sizes and applications.

## The approach

The identity is built around a mascot: a small, friendly robot whose visor is a games-controller
face, with a d-pad on one side and coloured buttons on the other. That makes it obviously about
games at a glance. Its rounded, chunky shapes keep it approachable, and the raised, waving hand
gives it personality.

I explored the robot in several colourways (teal, pink and pale blue) before settling on a
navy-and-white final design. It's paired with a bold, rounded orange wordmark, so the logo
uses two complementary brand colours.

## The system

- **Main logo**: the waving mascot beside the full "BitBoi Games" wordmark.
- **Variations**: a head-only "BBG" mark for small spaces such as app icons or avatars, a
  full-body "BBG" mark, a stacked lockup, and a version with orange headphones for use on blue
  backgrounds.
- **Business card**: the mascot on navy, framed by orange circuit-board traces that bring in
  the digital theme.

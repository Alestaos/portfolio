Part of the promo series for **XNDK**, a fictional co-ed K-pop band I created for my degree.
Their debut album is *Dreams and Nightmares* and its title track is *Blurred Love*. The band
blends Eastern flow and beats with Western lyrical storytelling. (See the
[Demi promo](/design/xndk-demi) for the full band concept.)

## Nari's promo

Nari's promo uses the series' **torn-paper reveal** in its boldest form. A cold, grey-scale
layer of windswept hair is ripped away across her face, and underneath she's in vivid colour,
with fiery copper hair, freckles and striking green eyes. Her colour is warm, while Demi's and
Kai's are cooler, so each member reads as an individual within the same system.

"Nari" is signed in orange script to match her hair.

Other members: [Demi](/design/xndk-demi) · [Xaveri](/design/xndk-xaveri) · [Kai](/design/xndk-kai).

## What this was

I was experimenting with **double exposure** and **lighting**: no client, just an excuse to
practise the technique and see how far the mood of an image could change.

## The pieces

Both studies use the same composition: a tight close-up of a cat's face, with the top of its
head dissolving into a treeline, so the forest seems to grow straight out of its fur and the
eyes are the strongest point in the frame.

The difference is the light:

- **Misty forest**: cool and desaturated, with fog rolling through the pines. The cat's amber
  eyes are the only warm colour, which makes them glow.
- **Sunlit forest**: the same idea pushed warm and golden, with low sun catching the tops of
  the trees. It's matched by the cat's green eyes, so the whole image feels like one sunlit
  scene.

Putting them side by side was the point of the exercise: the blend and composition stay the
same, and the lighting alone changes the mood from eerie to peaceful.

## The brief

Design a new homepage for **Mersus Technologies** that shows them as a leader in immersive VR
training and puts their flagship platform, **Avatar Academy**, front and centre. It needed to
feel as cutting-edge as the product, while still giving prospective clients the proof and
information they need to get in touch.

## The design

A dark, neon-lit interface in Mersus navy and cyan, with glowing outlines, angular "HUD" frames
and wireframe imagery, so the site itself feels like stepping into VR. The page is built as a
clear sales story from top to bottom:

1. **Hero.** The Avatar Academy logo and "Award-Winning Immersion" beside a glowing wireframe
   figure reaching out in a headset.
2. **Who We Are and Our Results.** A short introduction next to four circular stat rings
   (skill retention, time saved, learner engagement, efficiency), so the proof comes straight
   after the promise.
3. **Why Avatar Academy.** Eight benefits arranged around a central headset portrait, from
   *Scalable Training* to *Analytics Driven*, leading to an "Explore Avatar Academy Now!" button.
4. **Case Studies.** Framed cards for real client projects (robotic arm and PLC training,
   Boston Scientific, and a featured First Polymer injection-moulding study) to show the
   platform working in industry.
5. **Latest from LinkedIn.** Live social posts, including content from the
   [social series](/design/mersus-06-the-future-is-next) I designed, to keep the page current.
6. **Contact and footer.** A simple enquiry form in the same glowing frames, plus the address
   and a strip of partner logos (Enterprise Ireland, Unity, ATU, Meta) for credibility.

## Outcome

Mersus adapted this design for their current live site at
[mersus.ie](https://mersus.ie/). It keeps the same overall structure: a results row, a "Why
Avatar Academy" features section, industry case studies, a contact form and a partner-logo
footer.

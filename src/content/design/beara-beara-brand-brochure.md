## The brief

Beara Beara makes handcrafted leather bags in a family-run workshop in Bolivia and sells them
from London. As the brand's in-house Marketing Executive, I designed a printed brochure we
could hand to potential partners and collaborators, so they'd understand who the brand is
before the conversation even starts.

## The approach

The brochure is a 12-page A5 booklet that I wrote, designed and laid out in Photoshop, and had
printed professionally. I made all the visuals myself, mainly from Beara Beara's own
photography, with AI tools used for some of the imagery. It
moves from the brand's story to its products and back to the people behind it: a welcome
from the founder, the journey from Bolivia to London, the five brand values, the workshop
team, the Handbag, Adventure and Accessories collections, the craftsmanship process, past
collaborations, and a back cover with a QR code to the full collection.

Two page styles alternate to keep the rhythm. Story and product pages use a warm cream stock
with dark serif headings and gold italic subheads. Pages about values and partnerships flip
to deep green with gold type, so they read as the brand's statements. A dark green footer
band with a small gold bag emblem ties every inside page together.

The layouts are built around the photography. The collection pages give each bag a full-height
portrait panel with its name in the footer, and the craftsmanship page breaks the process into
four numbered steps. The penultimate page is left almost empty on purpose, to give the reader
a pause before the call to action on the back cover.

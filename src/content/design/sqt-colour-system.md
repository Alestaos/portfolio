## The brief

SQT Training needed its brand colours written down in one place, so course materials, social
media and marketing all use the same palette. I designed the colour system page for SQT's
visual identity guidelines.

## The approach

The page is built around SQT's two primary colours, SQT Green and Ocean Blue, set as large
slanted panels with their hex codes. These echo the angled shapes used across the brand.
Five secondary and supporting colours (Deep Green, Soft Sage, Sky Blue, Gold and Off-White) and
three neutral greys sit beside them as smaller swatches with matching slants.

A short usage note sets out the rules. The primary colours carry the key brand touchpoints,
while the supporting colours and neutrals add hierarchy and balance across digital and print.
The charcoal background and big white headline match the rest of the guidelines.

## About XNDK

**XNDK** is a fictional music band I created for my degree, a new act trying to break through
in both domestic and international markets with their debut album ***Dreams and Nightmares***
and its title track ***Blurred Love***. The name is an acronym of the four members: **X**averi,
**N**ari, **D**emi and **K**ai.

Their selling points are that they're **co-ed**, which makes them one of only two active K-pop
bands set up this way, and that they **merge Eastern and Western influences**, bringing Eastern
flow and beats to Western-style lyrical storytelling.

## Demi's promo

Each member gets a widescreen promo built on a shared idea: a **torn-paper reveal**. Demi's
portrait is mostly black and white, but strips of the image are torn back to show her in full
colour underneath, across one eye and her lips. It hints at the album's theme of dreams and
nightmares, and at the band's two worlds blending.

Her name is signed in pink handwritten script, giving each member their own accent colour,
while the spaced "X N D K" letters tie the series together.

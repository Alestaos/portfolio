## The brief

A **UCD Professional Diploma in Graphic Design** assignment: design a movie poster for a film
of my own creation. I wrote a crime thriller called **Nightwalker** and gave it the tagline:

> One man, one night, two lives.

## The approach

The poster sells the mood before anything else. A single hooded figure fills the frame with
his head bowed and his face half hidden, lit from below in a hot neon pink that makes him feel
both dangerous and haunted. Behind him a purple, star-flecked sky turns into a city skyline
glowing red on one side and blue on the other. The split hints at the "two lives" in the
tagline and suggests the police lights of a crime thriller.

The **Nightwalker** title uses a sharp, angular display face with a glitch of red and cyan on
the final letters, as if the image is breaking up. The tagline sits small and spaced at the
foot of the poster, the traditional place for a movie line, so the image and title get all
the attention.

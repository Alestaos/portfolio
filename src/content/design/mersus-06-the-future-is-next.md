Part of a series of social posts I designed for **Mersus Technologies**, a company building
virtual-reality training, and its platform **Avatar Academy**.

## The idea

Create the strongest sense of the future in the set: the feeling that VR changes how you see.

## The design

An extreme close-up of a human eye fills the vertical frame. Layered over the iris is a
holographic heads-up display of concentric cyan rings, ticks and light streaks, as if the
interface lives in the wearer's vision. The headline uses the same two-tier type as the rest
of the series, with a thin, spaced "The future is" over a huge cyan "**NEXT**".

I produced four layout variants to test where the type sits best against the eye, adjusting
the headline position, the crop and how the Mersus wordmark is set (stacked or in one line).
They're shown side by side here.

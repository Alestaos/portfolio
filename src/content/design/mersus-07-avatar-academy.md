Part of a series of social posts I designed for **Mersus Technologies**, a company building
virtual-reality training, and its platform **Avatar Academy**.

## The idea

Advertise **Avatar Academy**, Mersus's VR training platform, in a creative way instead of with
a standard product screenshot.

## The design

The Avatar Academy logo and the tagline "The Future of Training" sit on the left in white and
cyan. The right side is taken up by a figure drawn in a single continuous neon line, wearing
a VR headset and glancing up at a few floating line-art forms. The figure is drawn the same way
as the platform's avatar visuals, so it suggests a person mid-transition into the virtual
world. I made two background treatments, one fading from navy into teal at the bottom and one
flat navy, to suit different placements.

Part of a series of social posts I designed for **Mersus Technologies**, a company building
virtual-reality training, and its platform **Avatar Academy**.

## The idea

Show how a VR experience is built, going from wireframe to finished world, using a simple
before-and-after.

## The design

Both posts look straight into the lenses of a headset from the wearer's point of view. In the
first, the scene in each lens is only glowing neon outlines, like the wireframe stage of
building 3D models. In the second, the same figures are fully coloured and shaded. Keeping the
headset, framing and "Limitless Potential" line identical across both lets the change in the
lenses tell the story, so the pair works as a carousel or as two posts back to back.

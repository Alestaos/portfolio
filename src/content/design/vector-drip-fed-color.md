## The piece

A second **vector album cover in Illustrator**, again built from pure geometry.

Two sets of diagonal candy stripes run in from the top corners and weave into a tartan-like
"V" where they cross, pointing straight down to the focal point: a faceted hexagon, like a gem
or a cube seen from above, in gold, slate blue and black. The deep maroon background keeps the
stripes bright, and the gem's flat facets suggest depth without any gradients.

The title, *Drip Fed Color*, sits underneath in the same light, spaced display style as the
rest of the set.

Part of a small set of vector pieces with
[Different Shades of Music](/design/vector-different-shades-of-music) and
[Valentine's Day Romance](/design/vector-valentines-day-romance).

Part of the promo series for **XNDK**, a fictional co-ed K-pop band I created for my degree.
(See the [Demi promo](/design/xndk-demi) for the full band concept.)

## The brief

Take the widescreen promo campaign (the four member promos and the album announcement) and
adapt it for social media, where the posts have to work as square tiles in a feed.

## The approach

Rather than shrinking the widescreen artwork into a square, I **recomposed** each one for the
1:1 format. The crops move in tight on the faces so the torn-paper reveals fill the frame and
still read on a phone. The type is rebuilt as a stacked lockup in the bottom-left corner: the
member's name in their signature script and accent colour, over a bold, wide-tracked **XNDK**
wordmark. In the widescreen versions the band letters are spread across the frame. Here a
single strong wordmark works as a consistent signature for the band's profile grid.

The album's double exposure gets the same treatment. Cropped closer on the profile, the dragon
and burning city become the focus of the image, with the XNDK mark tucked into the corner.
Posted together, the five tiles form a cohesive launch grid for the band.

Part of a series of social posts I designed for **Mersus Technologies**, a company building
virtual-reality training, and its platform **Avatar Academy**.

## The idea

Explain, at a glance, what working with Mersus looks like for a customer, from the first
conversation to VR training running in their business.

## The design

An illustrated user in an Avatar Academy headset, holding controllers, sits at the centre on a
bright cyan circle. Dashed connector lines lead out to four steps, each with its own line icon
and a short, rhythmic line of copy:

- **Consult**: clients bring challenges, Mersus proposes VR solutions.
- **Analyse**: data flows, insights grow, performance shows.
- **Develop**: ideas are developed, prototypes refined, feedback embraced.
- **Deploy**: ready, trained, deployed, VR in action.

The Mersus Technologies wordmark and a cyan "Vision-to-Reality Process" title sit across the
top, in the same navy-and-cyan palette as the rest of the series.

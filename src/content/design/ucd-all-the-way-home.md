## The brief

Made for the photo-manipulation and digital-editing module of my **UCD Professional Diploma in
Graphic Design**. I set my own premise: someone missing their home. I framed the piece as a
vinyl cover for a song called **"All the Way Home"**.

## The idea

A man stands alone on a hilltop at night, looking up at the sky and dreaming of the home he's
left behind. The sky doesn't show what's really there. It shows what's in his head: a blazing
galaxy, a rocket rising towards it, and a city hanging upside down from the top of the frame,
as if home is somewhere up among the stars and just out of reach.

## The execution

The scene is built from several images blended into one: the silhouette, the night landscape,
the starfield, the rocket and the inverted cityscape. Warm oranges and magentas in the galaxy
contrast with the cold, dark foreground, so the eye goes from the small figure up into the
dream. The title runs in quiet, widely spaced capitals along the bottom edge, leaving the image
to do the work, as a record sleeve should.

A [special edition](/design/ucd-all-the-way-home-special) cover reworks the same premise.

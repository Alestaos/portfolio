Part of the promo series for **XNDK**, a fictional co-ed K-pop band I created for my degree.
Their debut album is *Dreams and Nightmares* and its title track is *Blurred Love*. (See the
[Demi promo](/design/xndk-demi) for the full band concept.)

## Kai's promo

Kai's promo is the coolest of the four, graded in steel blues and wintry greys, with him
wrapped in a heavy scarf against a cold backdrop. The series' **torn-paper reveal** cuts across
the lower half of his face, revealing a warmer layer underneath. The contrast between cold
outside and warmth beneath fits the album's *Dreams and Nightmares* theme.

"Kai" is signed in icy cyan script, completing the set of four member accent colours.

Other members: [Demi](/design/xndk-demi) · [Nari](/design/xndk-nari) · [Xaveri](/design/xndk-xaveri).

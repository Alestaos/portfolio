## About this piece

Jessica was one of my dogs growing up. A German shepherd, and I loved that dog to bits.

I took this one in low light. Rather than fight the grain, I leaned into it with warm colour and a cinematic letterbox.

Not every piece in this archive is about the technique. This one is about Jessica. My other childhood dog, [Maxie](/design/deviantart-era/maxie), is in here too.

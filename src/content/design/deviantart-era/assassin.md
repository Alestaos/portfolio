## About this piece

A single-colour study: everything is pushed into greens, with a fine grid texture and a burst of white lines behind the character to give the flat banner some energy.

## Sources

The character artwork is third-party anime-style illustration, not mine. My part was the cropping, layering, textures, lighting and type around it.

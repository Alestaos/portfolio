Part of a series of social posts I designed for **Mersus Technologies**, a company building
virtual-reality training, and its platform **Avatar Academy**.

## The idea

Show what VR makes possible without showing a headset at all. The post works like a daydream:
a person looks up, and the space above their head opens into a night sky full of places and
experiences they could step into.

## The design

The photo of the man is cut into a rounded starfield silhouette, so the cosmos seems to come
out of his imagination. Glowing cyan line drawings (a dragon, an astronaut, athletes, an
archer, and a child floating in a headset) circle the sky like constellations. A neon
"Limitless Potential" headline in the same line style tops it off, with the Mersus wordmark
anchoring the bottom corner.

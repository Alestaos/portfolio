Part of a series of social posts I designed for **Mersus Technologies**, a company building
virtual-reality training, and its platform **Avatar Academy**.

## The idea

Show the moment a real person is captured digitally: a wireframe laid over them, mapping their
features so they can be recreated in VR.

## The design

In the first post, a triangulated cyan face-tracking mesh sits over a smiling employee's face,
like scanning software at work. It's placed between the words "Training" and "**Evolution**",
so the person is literally at the centre of the change. The second post takes the idea further
by rebuilding a raised hand entirely from circuit-board traces, with a teal-to-magenta gradient
running from fingertips to wrist, under the line "Training Reimagined".

Part of a series of social posts I designed for **Mersus Technologies**, a company building
virtual-reality training, and its platform **Avatar Academy**.

## The idea

Show the link between a real employee and their digital avatar, the core of how Mersus trains
people in VR. It's the same person in both worlds.

## The design

The image is a mirror: the real employee at the top, and below, flipped upside down like a
reflection, his stylised 3D avatar wearing the same headset with the same beard and shirt.
Where the two halves meet, a cyan pixel-glitch band carries the line **"Training Reimagined"**,
as if the person is being digitised into VR right at that seam.

## The brief

SQT Training's Insights page lists articles in date order, with categories and a long
year-by-month archive underneath. Visitors have to scroll the whole feed before they can narrow
it down. In my role as Multimedia Content Developer, I mocked up a redesign of the page as a
contained first step, before any wider changes to the site.

## The approach

The concept turns the page from a blog index into a knowledge hub that leads with topics.
A green hero introduces the hub, followed by two hand-picked featured pieces and an Explore by
Topic row of filters near the top, so visitors can jump to their industry straight away.
Latest Insights shows three recent articles from different areas. A Downloadable Resources
section gives guides and checklists a home of their own, and the long archive becomes four
period buttons.

The site's existing header, footer and branding stay as they are, so the redesign fits the
current site and can be built inside the existing WordPress setup. This is a V1 concept with
placeholder text and imagery. It hasn't been built or published.

Part of a series of social posts I designed for **Mersus Technologies**, a company building
virtual-reality training, and its platform **Avatar Academy**.

## The idea

Let the audience see what someone in a headset is seeing. Normally that's hidden from anyone
watching. Turning the front of the headset into a window into the virtual world makes the
experience visible.

## The design

Two portraits of professionals in business wear, each wearing a headset whose front panel is
composited with the virtual scene inside: a bright, collaborative meeting room in one, and a
team of avatars against a city skyline in the other. The muted, desaturated portraits make the
colourful VR worlds stand out. Each post closes with a two-tier headline, a thin, widely
spaced lead-in over a bold cyan payoff ("See what's **Next**" and "The future is **Here**").

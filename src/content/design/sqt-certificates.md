## The brief

SQT Training issues certificates to every learner who attends or passes a course, including
its CQI | IRCA certified auditor training. I designed certificate layouts that carry the SQT
brand and fit the details each certificate needs.

## The approach

The Certificate of Achievement comes in two layouts. One leads with a deep green header and
a bold "Certificate of Achievement" headline, with the learner, course and details below. The
other is centred and lighter, with the round SQT logo at the top and a faint watermark behind the
learner's name. Both use the brand's greens and include the completion date, certificate and
learner numbers, the CQI | IRCA certified course mark and the director sign-offs.

The attendance certificate is more decorative. Green and gold curves frame the page, and a soft
grey riverside skyline sits behind the signatures.

Learner names, certificate and learner numbers, and signatures have been replaced or blurred
for this portfolio.

## The piece

A seasonal **Valentine's Day post** illustrated in **Illustrator**.

Two hands rise from the bottom of the frame, cupped as if offering something precious. They
hold two hearts: a solid rose-pink heart overlapped by a deep-red outline heart edged in a
row of dots, so the pair reads as two people linked together. A couple of small star accents
add a little sparkle. Everything sits on a soft blush-to-white gradient, which keeps the mood
gentle and romantic rather than loud.

The title, *Valentines Day Romance*, sits underneath in the same spaced display style as the
rest of the set.

Part of a small set of vector pieces with
[Different Shades of Music](/design/vector-different-shades-of-music) and
[Drip Fed Color](/design/vector-drip-fed-color).

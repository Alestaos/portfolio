## The piece

An album cover I created as a **vector illustration in Illustrator**, built entirely from
simple geometric shapes.

The composition centres on a set of concentric circles that suggest a vinyl record and its
label. It's sliced through by bold diagonal bands and scattered rounded squares, so the record
seems to break into overlapping planes. The palette sticks to a few closely related tones
(plum, blush pink, lavender and deep navy), so the "different shades" in the title are
literally the subject. Each overlap produces a new tint of the same family.

The title, *Different Shades of Music*, sits underneath in a light, spaced display face, keeping
the focus on the artwork.

Part of a small set of vector pieces with [Drip Fed Color](/design/vector-drip-fed-color) and
[Valentine's Day Romance](/design/vector-valentines-day-romance).

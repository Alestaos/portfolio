## The brief

Design a gift card for Beara Beara that captures the brand itself: vintage, handmade and
leather. A gift card is often a customer's first contact with the brand, so it had to feel as
considered as the bags it pays for.

## The approach

The whole card is treated as a piece of tooled leather. A rich brown hide texture sits under a
gold inset border with notched corners, like the edge stitching and embossing on a real bag.
The type is set to look gold-foiled: the "Gift Card" title in wide-spaced serif capitals, and
"Handmade • Timeless • Built to last" along the bottom. The white Beara Beara script logo is
the brightest element on the front. A brass wax-seal stamp reading "Handcrafted with care"
adds a tactile, heritage detail.

The card was built in Illustrator and Photoshop, with LTX Studio's AI tools used for the
detailed leather and brass work on the illustrated satchel and the seal.

The back keeps the same materials but is designed to be used. It has gold-ruled For and
Amount lines and a framed Code box that can be filled in by hand, plus a short panel
explaining how to redeem the card at checkout on bearabeara.co.uk.

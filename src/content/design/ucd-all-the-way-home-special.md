## The brief

A companion to the [normal edition](/design/ucd-all-the-way-home) cover, made for the same
photo-manipulation and digital-editing module of my **UCD Professional Diploma in Graphic
Design**. It's the special edition of the same imagined single, **"All the Way Home"**.

## The idea

The same story of home, told from the other direction. Instead of someone looking up and
dreaming, a girl is already on her way. She swims up and out of the city she grew up in,
through a sea that turns into a galaxy, towards a new home somewhere above.

## The execution

The frame is split vertically between the two homes: a dense, lit-up night skyline at the
bottom, and a bright rift of light at the top where the water opens into space. The swimmer
is a small silhouette between them, which makes the distance feel huge. Water, stars and
nebula colour are blended so it's hard to tell where the sea ends and the sky begins. The
title moves to the top edge, mirroring the normal edition, so the two covers read as a pair.

Part of the promo series for **XNDK**, a fictional co-ed K-pop band I created for my degree.
(See the [Demi promo](/design/xndk-demi) for the full band concept.)

## The brief

Announce the band's debut album, ***Dreams and Nightmares***, and drive pre-saves.

## The approach

The album title is a pair of opposites, so the promo is too. It's built on a **Photoshop double
exposure**: a portrait in profile on a clean white background, with two other worlds blended
into his silhouette. In his hair is a deep-blue night sky with a dragon coiled through it, the
*dream*. Through his neck and shoulders is a burning orange city skyline, the *nightmare*.

The type follows the same split. **"Dreams"** sits on the left with a small "Xndk" signature
underneath, and **"Nightmares"** on the right with the "Presave" call to action. The figure
stands between the two words, like the album's two halves meeting in one person. The bright
white background is a deliberate break from the moodier member promos, so the album
announcement stands out from the rest of the campaign.

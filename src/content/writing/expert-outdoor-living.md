## The brief

Drive online sales of Hartman's Eden outdoor furniture range for Expert Electrical in Monaghan
through paid social, as part of my in-house E-Commerce & Digital Marketing role with Flemings
Department Store. These were high-ticket garden sets, so each ad had to make the saving obvious
at a glance and point straight to the online store.

## The approach

Designed one template in the 4:5 feed format and ran it across the range: an "Outdoor Living"
kicker, the set's name in wide-tracked capitals, a short positioning line (for example "The
Spacious Entertainer" or "The Ultimate Outdoor Dining Experience"), and the now-versus-was
price as the bottom anchor, with the old price struck through. A single amber accent ties the
rule and the sale price together, and the lifestyle photography is darkened at the top and
bottom so the text stays readable over busy patio shots.

## Where it ran

Paid social placements, linking through to the Eden range on the online store.

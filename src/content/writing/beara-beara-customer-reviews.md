## The brief

Make more of the positive reviews Beara Beara's customers were leaving. Five-star feedback
does more for a handcrafted leather brand than its own claims about itself, but on a review
platform few people see it. The goal was to bring that praise into the brand's own channels.

## The approach

Turned individual reviews into designed content, with the customer's words as the headline
and the product they bought beside them. The social posts pair a styled product shot with the
quote on a soft cream panel, using the Beara Beara script logo, a "Loved by our customers"
kicker and five stars. A torn-paper edge and dried flowers give them a tactile, scrapbook
feel. The collage variant adds a lifestyle shot and a close-up of the leather and hardware,
so one review can carry several posts.

For the website, the same review became a "Customer Notes" card: just the quote, set in a
classic serif on aged paper inside an ornate frame, under the brand line "Love the past, wear
the future". It is sized to sit in the site's layout and stays readable on mobile.

## Where it ran

Organic posts on Beara Beara's social channels, with the review cards also shown on the
website.

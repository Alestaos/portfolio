## The brief

Beara Beara needed something light to leave behind: a single sheet that introduces the brand
to potential stockists and collaborators in the time it takes to read one side. As the
brand's in-house Marketing Executive, I wrote and designed it and had it printed
professionally for our outreach. I made all the visuals myself, mainly from Beara Beara's own
photography, with AI tools used for some of the imagery.

## The approach

The flyer shares its front with the brand brochure, so the two read as a set: the bags on a
stone ledge in front of Westminster, the script logo with "London" between gold rules, and
"Handmade Leather Bags" in wide-spaced capitals. A small gold QR code sits in the corner for
anyone who wants to see more.

The back is set on an aged parchment texture to match the brand's vintage feel. "Who We Are"
tells the story in four short paragraphs: a family-run leather workshop in Bolivia, a base in
London, and bags made to last. "What We Sell" then shows the range in three rounded photos,
from a backpack to two handbags. The address and website close the page under a small gold
bag emblem.

## The brief

The Road Safety Authority's **Check It Fits** team was coming to Flemings Department Store in
Monaghan on Tuesday 28 October 2025, offering free drop-in child car seat checks in the store
car park from 9am to 5:30pm. The aim was to get as many parents, guardians and parents-to-be as
possible to turn up on the day, and to support the Nursery Department's event-day offer of 10%
off car seats.

## The approach

Built a single key visual and adapted it to every placement rather than designing each one
separately. A warm, reassuring photo of a parent fastening a child's seatbelt carries the
message, with the official RSA Check It Fits mark top right. The bright amber headline,
"Does it fit? We'll check!", turns the safety message into an invitation, and the location and
date sit underneath in plain white so the essentials can't be missed.

The photo was recomposed for each aspect ratio so the two faces and the buckle stay in shot:

- **1:1 square** for social feeds
- **9:16 vertical** for TikTok and Stories, with extra headroom above the subjects
- **A4 poster** for in-store print, with an extra "RSA free Check It Fits at" line, because a
  poster is read without a caption beside it

## Where it ran

Across Flemings' social channels, on printed posters in-store, and alongside an events post on
the Flemings website with the full event details.

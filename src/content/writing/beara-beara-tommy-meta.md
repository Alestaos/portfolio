## The brief

Launch a paid campaign on **Meta** (Facebook and Instagram) for **Tommy**, a Beara Beara
leather backpack. It had to sell one product to a broad audience and show it as a bag that
works everywhere, from the commute to a weekend away.

## The idea

**City to wild.** The campaign is built on two settings: cobbled old-town streets for everyday
life, and misty highland lochs for weekend escapes. The bag travels between them unchanged,
which is the whole selling point. The opening pair of ads works as one sentence across two
cards: *"From city streets…"* / *"…to weekend escapes."*

## The approach

Every image is graded to **black and white except the bag**, which stays in its warm tan leather.
It's a simple, strong device: wherever the viewer looks, in a busy feed of colourful ads, the
product is the only colour on screen. The monochrome also gives the campaign a timeless,
editorial feel that suits a handcrafted heritage brand.

The set mixes shot types so the ads can be rotated and tested against each other:

- **Close product shots** that show the leather, stitching and brass buckles (*"Meet Tommy"*,
  *"Practical. Polished. Ready."*)
- **Wide lifestyle scenes** that put the bag into a story (*"Carry it your way"*, *"Built for
  everyday journeys"*)

Copy is kept to a few words in a light, widely spaced serif, so it supports the image
without competing with it.

## Where it ran

Paid placements across Meta (Facebook and Instagram) in the square feed format.

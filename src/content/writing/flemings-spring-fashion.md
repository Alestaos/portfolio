## The brief

As in-house E-Commerce & Digital Marketing Executive for Flemings Department Store, keep the
fashion department's social and email content fresh season to season while staying
recognisably "Flemings" across every post — including the third-party clothing brands the
store stocks, like Red Button, Fransa and White Stuff.

## The approach

Built a reusable ad template — a softly bordered product shot, a hand-lettered seasonal
headline over a clean serif category name, and the store URL anchoring the bottom — so new
fashion drops could be turned around quickly without redesigning from scratch each time.
Spring's run covered denim, dresses, knitwear and jackets, plus dedicated feature treatments
for individual brands carried in-store.

## Where it ran

Posted across Flemings' Instagram and Facebook feeds and folded into the seasonal email
newsletter, keeping product imagery consistent whether a customer saw it in their inbox or
their feed.

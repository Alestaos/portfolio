## The brief

Beara Beara reached out to cafés about brand collaborations. As the brand's in-house
Marketing Executive, I designed a printed brochure to open those conversations, so a café
owner could see what a partnership might look like before we'd even met.

## The approach

The brochure is an A4 sheet folded to A5, printed professionally. The outside works as a
calling card. The front is a warm café scene with a Beara Beara handbag beside a coffee in a
branded leather sleeve, under the series title "Crafted Connections". The back gives the pitch
in three short lines, a pencil-style London skyline, and a dark green contact panel with the
website, socials, my details as the brand contact, and a QR code to the collection.

Inside, the spread turns the idea into specifics. "In-Café Displays" suggests a curated
selection of three to five pieces on existing shelving, backed by four benefits: a tailored
display, exclusive QR customer offers, personalised embossing and bespoke products. "Coffee
Loyalty Sleeves" pitches co-branded leather cup sleeves as a loyalty reward, with concept
visuals showing a café's own name embossed in leather and a sleeve that reads "Coffee: Because
Adulting is Hard". A "Have a different idea?" panel leaves the door open for the café's own
suggestions.

The café and hotel brochures share one layout system, so they read as a series: the same
cover structure, cream and dark green palette, and contact panel, with the copy and imagery
changed for each audience.

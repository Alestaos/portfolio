## The brief

A **candidate task** set by Flemings of Monaghan as part of their hiring process: create a
promotional piece for **Nike** sportswear, one of the brands the store carries. It was a
speculative concept for the application, not a campaign commissioned or run by Nike.

## The idea

**"Break Your Barriers."** Every runner has a wall, whether that's a pace, a distance or the
voice telling them to stop. The promo shows that wall breaking: the runner is caught mid-stride
as a barrier of glass explodes around him, as if he's just run straight through it.

## The execution

The runner is cut out against a plain concrete wall, a deliberately cold, grey, urban setting.
Shards of glass burst outwards from behind him, each one edged in electric teal so the impact
feels like energy rather than damage. The glow picks up the blue of his kit, tying runner and
effect together, and a soft rim light lifts him off the background.

The headline is set in tall, condensed navy capitals in the open space on the right, so it
reads cleanly against the concrete. The Nike swoosh sits alone in the bottom corner, a
confident, minimal sign-off that suits the brand.

## The brief

Grow Mersus Technologies' organic social presence through 2024 and 2025 and drive traffic to
its weekly blog. The blog covered where VR came from, how avatar-based learning works, and what
VR training is doing in fields like healthcare, education and industry. Each post had to make a
fairly technical topic feel approachable and give people a clear reason to click through.

## The approach

Built a recognisable, repeatable system so a new post could go out every week without
redesigning from scratch:

- **A strong two-tone headline.** A short white lead-in ("VR in", "Virtual Reality") over a
  huge condensed word in Mersus cyan, often split across two colours ("HEALTH**CARE**",
  "EDU**CATION**") so the key topic jumps out of the feed.
- **Moody, colour-washed photography.** Real people in headsets, toned down into Mersus navy so
  the type and frame always stay readable.
- **A bracket frame with angled slashes.** White and cyan line work frames the headline and
  gives every post the same dynamic, techy edge.
- **A clear call to action.** A one-line teaser for the blog post, a "Read Now!" button or
  mersus.io tag, and a footer of Mersus's three pillars: Avatar Academy, VR Training and
  Immersive Developer.

Once the template was in place, a new topic only needed a new image, headline and teaser, as
the Healthcare, Education and Success Stories posts show. The more editorial pieces, like
*Virtual Reality Evolution* and *Avatar Learning*, varied the layout while keeping the same
palette and type.

## Where it ran

Mersus Technologies' organic social channels, each post linking to that week's article on the
Mersus blog.

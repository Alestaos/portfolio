## The concept

"Freddy's Adventures" — a 12-part mystery series tracking Freddy, Flemings' toy-department elf,
through December. He breaks loose on the 1st, gets into increasingly ridiculous trouble around
the store, and is finally caught on the 24th with customers' help, tying into an in-store "find
Freddy" game and the @flemingstoymaster social tag.

## The format

Each episode pairs a short AI-generated "breaking news" anchor segment with real handheld
footage shot inside the store, cut together in a black-and-white "CCTV" style with on-screen
captions — a cheap, fast way to produce a long-running series without a studio shoot for every
instalment.

## Where it ran

Published across social, folded into the store's email newsletter, and collected on a dedicated
page on the Flemings estore so customers could follow the whole series in order.

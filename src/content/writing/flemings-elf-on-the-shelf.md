## The concept

Freddy, Flemings' toy-department mascot elf, fronting a recurring "breaking news" bit for the
Christmas run-up — mock live-report graphics, a straight-to-camera delivery, and a running gag
about Freddy getting into trouble and being sent back to Santa.

## The format

Shot and edited as short-form video for Instagram and Facebook — vertical, built to land in a
single watch rather than a static ad, with a consistent news-report graphic treatment carried
across the run.

## The brief

Make the business case for VR training to the decision-makers who read LinkedIn: HR and L&D
leads, operations managers and business owners. Mersus Technologies needed a piece that could
be taken in quickly in the feed, but that still had enough substance to show why companies
should invest in immersive training and in its platform, Avatar Academy.

## The approach

A tall, scroll-friendly infographic built around one simple idea: **four benefits, in order.**

- **The headline.** An oversized cyan "4" anchors tall, widely spaced condensed type ("Benefits
  of VR Training") so the promise is clear before anyone starts reading.
- **The intro.** A short paragraph frames the claims as Mersus's own observations from client
  feedback, with the Mersus Technologies and Avatar Academy logos side by side underneath.
- **The path.** A single cyan line winds down the page, switching sides at each step, so
  readers' eyes zig-zag through the four numbered points. That rhythm suits a vertical scroll
  on mobile.
- **Each benefit** gets a bold condensed heading, one plain-language line, and one supporting
  statistic or concrete payoff: training time cut by 40–60% (**Accelerated Learning**),
  knowledge retention up by 80% (**Increased Retention**), risk-free hazardous training
  (**Enhanced Safety**), and savings on travel, equipment and instructors (**Cost Efficient**).

A dark navy background with faint circuit-board traces keeps it on-brand with the rest of
Mersus's content and fits the subject, without competing with the copy.

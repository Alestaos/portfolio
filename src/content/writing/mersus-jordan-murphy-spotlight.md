## The brief

Put a human face on Mersus Technologies by telling the stories of the people behind its VR
training. This spotlight featured **Jordan Murphy**, the Platform Lead for Avatar Academy, the
company's flagship VR training platform.

## The writing

I wrote the blog feature *"Jordan Murphy – Leading Innovation with Avatar Academy"* (published
11 April 2025). It follows Jordan's journey from Game Art and Design graduate to joining Mersus
through its graduate programme on the strength of his final-year project. From there he became
Platform Lead and drove Avatar Academy's move to a single unified application in six months
instead of the planned eighteen.

The piece is split into short sections (*From Graduate to Leader*, *Pioneering the Avatar
Academy Platform*, *Beyond the Code*) and ends with a quick-read *Fast Facts About Jordan*.
That way it works both for skimmers and for people reading the whole profile, and it shows
Mersus as a place where graduates are trusted to lead.

## The promo banner

To promote the post, I designed a 16:9 banner in the same navy-and-cyan system as the rest of
Mersus's social content. Jordan's name is set huge in white on the left over a faint
topographic texture, and a cut-out photo of him with arms crossed stands against a bold cyan
panel on the right. A one-line hook ("From graduate to platform lead…") and the mersus.io URL
give people a reason to click through.

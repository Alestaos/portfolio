## The brief

Introduce Beara Beara to a LinkedIn audience and give the background behind the brand: who it
is, where its bags come from, and why that matters. That story is a lot to fit into one post,
and on LinkedIn it has to stand out in a feed of corporate graphics.

## The approach

Designed the post as the front page of an old broadsheet, so it reads as a story rather than
an ad. A heavy serif masthead spells out "Beara Beara", flanked by "Est. 2012" and "Handmade to
last" ear panels. A dateline-style strapline carries the brand promise: "Independently
designed. Ethically made. Handmade to last."

Below it, four photo columns each tell one part of the story, with a newspaper headline and a
short paragraph:

- **We Are Beara Beara**: the London shopfront, introducing a London design house that brings
  together British design and Bolivian craftsmanship
- **Designed for People**: the Athena handbag worn out and about
- **A Family, Not a Factory**: the leatherworkers who make the bags, and the generations of
  skill behind them
- **Designed for Real Life**: the Noah satchel as an everyday bag for work, study and
  commuting

All the photography is toned to sepia, set on creased, aged newsprint and finished with
period rules and a "BB" monogram, so the whole post feels like a piece of the brand's
heritage. The footer repeats the brand's key points: small batch production, real leather,
built to last.

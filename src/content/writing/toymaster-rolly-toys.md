## The brief

Promote the **Rolly Toys** range of ride-on tractors in the toy department at Flemings, sold
under Toymaster, and make it feel exciting to the kids it's aimed at and practical to the
parents buying.

## The approach

Designed the poster in **Photoshop** around a single hero product, a red ride-on tractor with a
working yellow front loader, set on a bold teal-green background like a field. Chunky yellow
tyre tracks run across a yellow header band carrying the Rolly Toys logo, and the headline
**"Your Adventure Starts Here!"** speaks straight to the child's imagination. The footer covers
what parents need: the Flemings brand, the website, and the humm logo to show that
buy-now-pay-later is available.

## Bringing it to life

To give the campaign more reach on social, I took the finished poster and **turned it into a
short video using AI**, animating the still artwork into motion. Moving content gets far more
attention in the feed than a static image, so one piece of artwork produced two assets.
